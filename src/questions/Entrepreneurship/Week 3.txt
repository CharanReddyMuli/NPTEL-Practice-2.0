Q: An effective Ideation facilitator would ideally be:
A) A computer analyst
B) An empathetic and creative thinker
C) A time conscious manager
D) A seasoned administrator
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: An effective facilitator understands people's perspectives and encourages creative thinking.

Q: What is a native prototype?
A) An improved product that is a hybrid of new and existing innards
B) A novel product developed on a zero-base approach
C) A product that reflects ethic nativity
D) A complete clone of an existing product
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A native prototype is defined here as a novel product developed from a zero-base approach.

Q: Which of the following is not a Smart Wearable?
A) Smart watch
B) Smart glass
C) Smart treadmill
D) Smart shoe
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: A smart treadmill is generally fitness equipment rather than a wearable device.

Q: An integrated model of prototype development requires:
A) Sequential design and manufacture
B) Diversified design and manufacture
C) Concurrent design and manufacture
D) Completely in-house manufacture
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Integrated prototype development involves design and manufacturing activities happening concurrently.

Q: Under the World Trade Organization’s TRIPS Agreement, patent protection should be available and respected across:
A) Only the state granting the patent
B) All signatory states
C) Signatory as well as non-signatory states
D) Neither signatory nor non-signatory states
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: TRIPS establishes minimum intellectual property protection standards for WTO members. Patents are territorial, however, so a patent granted in one country does not automatically apply in every member country.

Q: Which patent of Amazon granted in 1999 enabled the company dominate online book retailing?
A) “One-click” online purchase system
B) “One-click” offline purchase system
C) “One-click” page search system
D) None of the above
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Amazon's one-click purchasing patent covered a streamlined online purchasing method.

Q: For the performance testing of a prototype to happen, which of the following is not required as much as the others?
A) Product specifications
B) Testing methods
C) Testing equipment
D) External look and feel of the product
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Performance testing primarily evaluates function and specifications rather than external appearance.

Q: Which of the following is a variable experience product?
A) Smart phone
B) Viewing movies from a movie library
C) Drinking water from a purifier
D) A luxury fashion accessory
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A movie library offers different experiences depending on the content selected by the viewer.

Q: The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) version of a smart watch, beyond its timekeeping function, must be:
A) Its colour scheme
B) Its strap
C) Its dial face
D) Its health parameter tracking
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Health parameter tracking provides a core additional function that distinguishes a smart watch from a conventional watch.

Q: Field testing of a product should be based on:
A) Repeat of lab testing
B) Simulated operating conditions
C) Destruction testing
D) Lifelong testing
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Simulated operating conditions help evaluate product behaviour in realistic use scenarios.

Q: What is the primary use of an Ideation Workshop?
A) Serve as a workshop for designing and developing prototypes
B) Serve as a platform for identifying problems and developing creative solutions
C) Serve as a methodology of formal day to day departmental meetings
D) None of the above
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Ideation workshops help participants identify problems, generate ideas, and develop creative solutions.

Q: In a smart wearable which of the following is likely to be a fundamental, first step, prototype?
A) Circuit engineering prototype
B) Communication prototype
C) Commercial prototype
D) All of the above
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: A circuit engineering prototype can establish the basic electronic functionality before communication features and commercial refinement.

Q: Patent granted for an article of manufacture is called a:
A) Product patent
B) Process patent
C) Composition of matter patent
D) All of the above
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: A product patent protects an invention embodied in a product, while process patents protect methods or processes.

Q: What is the definition of value proposition for a customer?
A) Costs and valuation of technology
B) Valuation of the company that manufactures the product
C) A proposal by the company how to value a product
D) A set of gains and benefits from the product for the customer
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: A value proposition explains the benefits and value a product offers to its customers.

Q: Validation accomplishes the following with respect to a product or service:
A) Establishes the basic product premise at the hands of users
B) Establishes the level of customer fulfillment
C) Provides guidance on the nexus between the product and the business
D) All of the above
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Validation checks product assumptions with users, measures customer needs and satisfaction, and helps assess business viability.
