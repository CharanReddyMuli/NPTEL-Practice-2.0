Q: Alexander Kritikos in his paper titled ”Entrepreneurs and their impact on jobs and economic growth” in IZA World of Labor (May 2014) used the following phrase to aptly describe the disruption of an industry caused by innovative entrepreneurs:
A) Hyper disruption
B) Hyper destruction
C) Creative destruction
D) Creative construction
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Creative destruction describes how innovation replaces outdated products, technologies, and business models with new ones.

Q: According to ILO statistics (2018), the percentage of employment in the formal sector is:
A) Less than 10%
B) Between 20 and 30%
C) Between 40 and 50%
D) Above 50%
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The accepted course answer is less than 10%, referring to the specific employment statistic cited in the course material.

Q: As industries mature:
A) Employment accelerates
B) Employment decelerates
C) Junior employees become less important
D) None of the above
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: As industries mature, productivity gains, automation, and slower expansion can reduce the rate of employment growth.

Q: Food delivery companies such as Zomato and Swiggy brought this change to the employment scenario:
A) Huge formal employment on fulltime rolls
B) Huge informal employment, all on contract
C) A mix of huge formal and informal employment, on a mix of fulltime and part-time rolls
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Food delivery platforms create different types of work, including salaried positions and contract-based delivery work.

Q: In terms of employment, entrepreneurship is nothing but:
A) A divine choice
B) A serendipitous choice
C) An occupational choice
D) A referral choice
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Entrepreneurship is an occupational choice in which individuals pursue self-employment or establish businesses.

Q: One of the greatest authors on employment and entrepreneurship who wrote a landmark book in 1934 was:
A) Rensis Likert
B) Joseph Schumpeter
C) Paul Samuelson
D) Abraham Maslow
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Joseph Schumpeter's work on economic development and entrepreneurship highlighted the role of innovation in economic change.

Q: Village industries (for example, pottery, carpentry, honey extraction) give self-employment to:
A) Farmers
B) ITI diploma holders
C) Skilled artisans, whether formally educated or not
D) Migrant labour

Q: E-Commerce start-up sector’s growth has triggered huge corresponding growth in:
A) Malls
B) Kirana stores
C) Logistics
D) Advertising
ANSWER: C

Q: Which of the following statements is true, with reference to a company?
A) The Board of Directors is not responsible for Corporate Governance
B) The CEO is not responsible but the Board of Directors is responsible for Corporate Governance
C) Corporate Governance is the responsibility of shareholders
D) The Board of Directors and the CEO are responsible for Corporate Governance
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The board provides oversight, while the CEO and management implement governance policies and controls.

Q: The following governmental agency takes a lead role in formulating policies for and supervising corporate governance of listed companies in India:
A) IRDA
B) RBI
C) SEBI
D) National Stock exchange
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: SEBI regulates India's securities markets and establishes governance requirements for listed companies.

Q: Entrepreneurship and economic growth have:
A) A dynamic relationship
B) A static relationship
C) A negative relationship
D) A random relationship
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Entrepreneurship can drive innovation, productivity, job creation, and economic growth, while economic conditions also influence entrepreneurship.

Q: Formalising employment has the advantages of:
A) Improving operational efficiency
B) Improving job security and working conditions
C) Facilitating reinvestment and growth
D) All of the above
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Formal employment can improve worker protections, organisational processes, and long-term business development.

Q: Which of the following is a characteristic of informal employment?
A) Guaranteed long-term employment
B) Formal social security benefits
C) Employment without formal contractual arrangements
D) Employment exclusively in government organizations
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Informal employment often lacks formal contracts, employment protections, and social security benefits.

Q: Which institution in India regulates corporate governance for listed companies?
A) OECD
B) ILO
C) IMF
D) SEBI
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: SEBI establishes and enforces corporate governance requirements for listed companies in India.

Q: Which of the following policies is related to corporate governance in a company?
A) Whistlestop policy
B) Whistlestart policy
C) Whisleblower policy
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: A whistleblower policy provides a channel for reporting suspected misconduct, fraud, or violations within an organisation.
