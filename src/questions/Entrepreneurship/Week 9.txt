Q: For Founder to CEO transition to be successful in a start-up, the CEO must possess, beyond, technical and business skills:
A) Ability to invest in the start-up
B) Ability to quit at any time
C) Cultural skills and fit
D) Constantly questioning attitude
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: A CEO needs to fit the organisation's culture and lead people effectively in addition to having technical and business skills.

Q: In terms of ensuring good corporate governance, the start-up must have the following from the very beginning:
A) A board comprising independent experts and leaders, including the founder and co-founder
B) An organisation structure
C) A recruitment procedure
D) A hire and fire policy
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: An effective board provides oversight, strategic guidance, accountability, and independent perspectives.

Q: One of the important characteristics even if for an all-powerful CEOs is to have the
A) The humility of advocacy
B) The consensus from all stakeholders
C) Both (a) and (b)
D) Neither (a) nor (b)
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Effective leadership requires humility, openness to advocacy, and efforts to build stakeholder consensus.

Q: Global concerns on climate change can lead to new start-up opportunities in:
A) Medtech
B) Edtech
C) Cleantech
D) Fintech
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Cleantech start-ups develop solutions for renewable energy, energy efficiency, pollution reduction, and climate challenges.

A) In select pockets of the value chain

Q: As a global trend, while the success in start-up will lead to exceptional rewards, start-ups do face:
A) Little or no failure rate
B) High failure rate
C) Governmental bailout even in failure

Q: Which is the right stage to induct professional leaders in a start-up?
A) Ideation stage
B) Prototype stage
C) Early growth stage
D) Maturity stage
ANSWER: C

Q: What is the typical Founder’s Dilemma?
