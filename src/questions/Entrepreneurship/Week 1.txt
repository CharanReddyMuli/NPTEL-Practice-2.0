Q: Most successful new-age start-ups in India deploy the following:
A) Imported technologies
B) Vintage technologies
C) Social technologies
D) Digital technologies
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Digital technologies help start-ups scale services, automate operations, and reach customers efficiently.

Q: Government of India has the following qualification criterion for an entity to be called as ‘startup’ in India, reckoned from the date of inception.
A) Age of the start-up
B) Age of the founders
C) Age of the investors
D) None of the above
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The age of the entity from its incorporation or registration is one of the criteria used in India's official start-up recognition framework.

Q: An entrepreneur who enters the market with a product or service which is based on a similar or improved product that is already existing is called:
A) Innovative entrepreneur
B) Replicative entrepreneur
C) Opportunity entrepreneur
D) Necessity entrepreneur
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A replicative entrepreneur adopts or improves an existing product, service, or business model.
