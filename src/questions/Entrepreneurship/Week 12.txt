Q: The ideal space for a start-up in the matrix of needs and futurism (the need-marketing pivot) is:
A) Linear Known
B) Linear Unknown
C) Non-linear Known
D) Non-linear Unknown
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Non-linear unknown spaces can offer opportunities to address emerging needs through innovative products and business models.

Q: Strategically, marketing is all about:
A) Selling products to consumers as much as possible
B) Pricing products at the highest possible level
C) Matching new company capabilities and new consumer needs
D) Convincing others
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Strategic marketing aligns a company's capabilities with customer needs to create value and competitive advantage.

Q: The quote “like all disciplines, entrepreneurship has models, processes and case studies.” Is by:
A) Donald F Kuratko
B) Peter Drucker
C) Eric Ries
D) Clayton Christensen
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Donald F. Kuratko is a prominent entrepreneurship scholar and author.

Q: Which of the following represents the highest level of need in Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs?
A) Safety needs
B) Social needs
C) Esteem needs
D) Self-actualisation needs
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Self-actualisation represents the pursuit of personal growth and the realisation of one's potential in Maslow's hierarchy.

Q: Chennai Business School was established by senior working executives and became successful despite competition. It demonstrates:
A) Opportunity Entrepreneurship
B) Replicative Entrepreneurship
C) Employee Entrepreneurship
D) Necessity Entrepreneurship
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Opportunity entrepreneurship involves identifying and pursuing a promising business opportunity.

Q: Every start-up will have its core competence but all successful start-ups have a unique additional competency:
A) Governmental support
B) Industry support
C) Ability to conceptualise and execute an integrated business strategy and business model
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Successful start-ups connect their core capabilities with a coherent business strategy and business model.

Q: The unique differentiating competence required for natural products to be medicines is the following:
A) Finance
B) The technical ability to characterise and isolate the active wellness or therapeutic ingredient
C) Being in the large-scale sector
D) None of the above
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Identifying and isolating active compounds helps establish a natural product's therapeutic properties and supports medicine development.

Q: Natsol Laboratories chose a natural product for its business. It is
A) Aspartime
B) Stevia
C) Sucralose
D) Ginseng
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Stevia is a plant-derived sweetener used as an alternative to conventional sugar.

Q: Which of the following is NOT one of the traditional 4 Ps of marketing?
A) Product
B) Price
C) Promotion
D) People
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The traditional 4 Ps are Product, Price, Place, and Promotion.

Q: Integrated Cleanroom Technology supplies its products primarily to:
A) Automobile industry
B) Chemical industry
C) Sterile pharmaceutical industry
D) Housing industry
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Cleanroom technology supports controlled environments required in sterile pharmaceutical manufacturing.

Q: The takeaway from Ricon Pharma is:
A) Technical competence provides resilience for start-ups to withstand business vicissitudes
B) Unanticipated environmental changes can impact business strategy
C) India offers competitive advantage to science and technology start-ups
D) All of the above
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The case highlights the importance of technical expertise, adapting to external changes, and leveraging India's scientific capabilities.

Q: Notion Press offers the following key takeaway:
A) Unfulfilled customer needs offer great opportunities for start-ups
B) There is potential in India for printing presses
C) Publishing can be automated
D) Every printing press can be a publishing house
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Notion Press addresses the needs of aspiring authors by making publishing services more accessible.

Q: Marketing mix is commonly referred to as:
A) ABC of marketing
B) 3 Rs of marketing
C) 4 Ps of marketing
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The marketing mix traditionally consists of Product, Price, Place, and Promotion.
