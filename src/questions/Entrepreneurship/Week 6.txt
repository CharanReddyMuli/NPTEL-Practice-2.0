Q: Completely autonomous vehicle is an example of
A) Inadequate perfection
B) Inadequate innovation
C) Imperfect innovation
D) Perfect innovation
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: A completely autonomous vehicle represents the ideal of fully automated driving, according to the course's innovation framework.

Q: Which industry in India has several hundreds of brands for the same generic product?
A) Cycle industry
B) Automobile industry
C) Pharmaceutical industry
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: India's pharmaceutical industry has numerous brands selling medicines with the same generic ingredients.

Q: Which of the following statements is relatively true?
A) Design is only an integrator of different functionalities into one product
B) Design is only a tool to promote different products with different functionalities
C) Design can integrate as well as differentiate functionalities in one or more products
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Design can combine multiple functions in one product or distinguish products through different features and experiences.

Q: Which of the following statements is true because of automobile electrification and autonomous driving?
A) There would be opportunities for several technology led startups in the new generation components
B) Automobile electrification and autonomy are hyped up and offer little opportunity to start-ups
C) New generation components can be easily done in the facilities of existing auto component makers
D) All of the above
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Electric and autonomous vehicles create opportunities in sensors, software, batteries, and other advanced components.

Q: What can additive manufacturing do to the automobile industry?
A) Add weight to components
B) Make components with just the required amount of materials
C) Improve look and feel
D) None of the above
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Additive manufacturing builds components layer by layer, helping reduce material waste and create optimised designs.

Q: From mobile phones, the recovery of which of the following critical materials presents a technological challenge and a potential start-up opportunity?

Q: Just-in-time inventory system is aligned with
A) Push-type demand system
B) Pull-type demand system
C) Econometric demand system
D) Expert demand system
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: A pull system replenishes inventory in response to actual demand rather than producing based only on forecasts.
