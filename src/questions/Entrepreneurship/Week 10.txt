D) Super salt

EXPLANATION: Tata Group developed Tata Neu as a digital super app that brings together services from multiple Tata businesses.

Q: Which of the following is true?
A) Employee entrepreneurs will carry out projects dictated by the employers only
B) Employee entrepreneurs may develop products useful for their companies or for broader industries
C) Employee entrepreneurs eventually give up on their projects
D) Organisations lose talent because of employee entrepreneurship
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Employee entrepreneurs, also called intrapreneurs, innovate within organisations and may create solutions useful beyond their immediate teams.
