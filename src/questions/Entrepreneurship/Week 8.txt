Q: PISA stands for:
A) Programme for International Start-up Assessment
B) Programme for International Student Assessment
C) Programme for Indian Student Assessment
D) Programme for Indian Start-up Assessment
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: PISA is the OECD's Programme for International Student Assessment, which evaluates students' skills and knowledge.

Q: Indian Government’s Make in India thrust is expected to benefit from India’s capabilities in:
A) Start-ups
B) Software
C) Frugal engineering
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Frugal engineering focuses on developing effective, affordable solutions while using resources efficiently.

Q: Which of the following is a key measure for assessing the impact of an entrepreneurial ecosystem?
A) Number of start-ups created
B) Commercial success
C) Industry linkages
D) All of the above
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: A strong entrepreneurial ecosystem can be assessed through start-up creation, business outcomes, and industry collaboration.

Q: Start-ups have a great opportunity to contribute to the ushering in of:
A) Circular economy
B) Declining economy
C) Volatile economy
D) None of the above
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Start-ups can support a circular economy through reuse, repair, recycling, and more efficient resource use.

Q: According to the dominant-discipline approach, start-ups can be specialised around which combination of disciplines?
A) Science, technology, mathematics and engineering
B) Finance, marketing, HR and operations
C) Arts, history, commerce and law
D) Economics, sociology, psychology and political science
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: The dominant-discipline approach can focus on STEM capabilities to build technology-driven start-ups.

Q: Globally, the following US universities are the most famous ones for nurturing a strong and vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem:
A) Arizona and Georgia
B) Stanford and MIT
C) Harvard and Columbia
D) Yale and Michigan

Q: To lead in analytics based start-ups, one needs a strong base of:
A) Arts
B) Geology
C) Mathematics
D) History
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Mathematics underpins statistics, probability, modelling, and other core analytics techniques.

Q: What is a Founder’s Dilemma?
A) Which product to choose
B) How much to invest
C) When to transfer leadership to professionals
D) Where to sell
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: The founder's dilemma includes deciding whether and when to retain control or hand leadership to professional managers.

Q: Co-founders of a successful start-up usually have:
A) Exactly same skills
B) Opposing attitudes
C) Complementary skills and attitudes
D) Same family lineage
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Complementary skills allow co-founders to cover different areas such as technology, business, operations, and sales.

Q: The succession sagas at Tata Group and Infosys emphasise that for harmonious transition when founders are still the investors or statured leaders, harmony is ensured by:
A) A complete break with the past
B) Having all key decisions formally vetted by the founders
C) Change with Continuity
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Change with continuity balances organisational renewal with the preservation of valuable culture, values, and experience.

Q: The Government of India had a goal of making India a ______ economy by 2025.
A) A 3 trillion dollar economy
B) A 5 trillion dollar economy
C) A 7 trillion dollar economy
D) A 9 trillion dollar economy
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: India set a policy ambition to become a $5 trillion economy. The 2025 date was a target, not confirmation that the goal was achieved by then.
