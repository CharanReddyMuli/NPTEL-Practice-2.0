Q: Computer software gets typically commercialised as:
A) Only B2B
B) Only B2C
C) Both B2B and B2C
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Software can be sold to businesses (B2B) as well as individual consumers (B2C).

Q: Start-ups become unicorns with their focus on:
A) Stable profits even at low scale
B) Rapid revenue growth and high market share
C) High pricing
D) None of the above
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Start-ups often pursue rapid growth and market expansion to achieve high valuations, although unicorn status specifically means a private start-up valuation of at least $1 billion.

Q: Successful commercialisation rests on which of the following?
A) Demand creation
B) Demand fulfillment
C) Customer loyalty
D) All of the above
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Commercial success depends on generating demand, fulfilling customer needs, and building customer loyalty.

Q: A disruptive innovation is a potent combination of:
A) High technology and Low cost
B) Creative business model and High technology
C) Creative business model and low cost
D) High technology, Low cost and Creative business model
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: The course framework describes disruption as combining technology, affordability, and an innovative business model.

Q: In the personal computer industry, disruption was hastened by:
A) Look and feel of the computers
B) Computer languages
C) Exponential increase in processing power of the chips
D) Input-Output media
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Rapid improvements in chip processing power helped make personal computers increasingly capable and affordable.
