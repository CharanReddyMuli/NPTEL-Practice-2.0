Q: Which type of innovation do start-ups primarily focus on?
A) Evolutionary Innovation
B) Sustainable Innovation
C) Disruptive Innovation
D) Incremental Innovation
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Start-ups often pursue disruptive innovation to introduce new solutions, business models, or markets.

Q: “Every company that has tried to manage mainstream and disruptive businesses within a single organization failed.” This quote is from an article in HBR authored by:
A) Eric Ries
B) Joseph L Bower and Clayton M Christensen
C) Michael Porter
D) Peter Drucker
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Joseph L. Bower and Clayton M. Christensen studied the challenges established companies face when managing disruptive innovation.

Q: A defining acquisition of Google that took the company to leadership in mobile telephone industry was:
A) YouTube
B) Android
C) Instagram
D) Nest
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Google's acquisition of Android helped it establish a major position in the mobile operating system market.

Q: The pace of technology development in modern times is characterised as:
A) Linear
B) Progressive
C) Non-linear
D) Non-Progressive
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Technological progress can accelerate through feedback loops and breakthroughs rather than proceeding at a constant rate.

Q: Just as chips revolutionised computing in the past (and will continue to), in future one of the following will revolutionise a whole spectrum of industries.
A) Radars
B) Lidars
C) Motors
D) Smart Sensors
ANSWER: D
EXPLANATION: Smart sensors combine sensing with processing and connectivity, enabling automation and intelligent systems across industries.

Q: In the Pioneering Leadership Model, which form of leadership is positioned at the top?
A) Cost Leadership
B) Differentiation Leadership
C) Innovation Leadership
D) Pioneering Advantage

EXPLANATION: Innovation leadership focuses on developing novel solutions and establishing new directions in the market.
