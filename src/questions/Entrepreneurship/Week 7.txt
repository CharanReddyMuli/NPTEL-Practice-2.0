Q: Which of the following is a financial strategy decision?
A) Diversification strategy decision
B) Integration strategy decision
C) Dividend decision
D) Recruitment strategy decision
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Dividend decisions determine how much of a company's earnings should be distributed to shareholders versus retained in the business.

Q: The following is important for smooth and prudential conduct of a company’s operations:
A) Periodic equity infusion
B) Debt on tap
C) Net positive cash flow from operations
D) None of the above
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Positive operating cash flow helps a company pay expenses and maintain sustainable operations.

Q: In the early phases of a start-up (say, ideation and prototyping), a start-up usually receives:
A) Angel funding, typically a form of seed-stage funding
B) Venture capital funding
C) Private equity funding
